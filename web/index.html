Listings

Ads of the same home (same address and size, or the same spot with the same size and rent) appear once; each source links to its ad. Tick "Show duplicate ads" to see them separately. "Listed" is the site's date, or when this app first saw the home.

Your current home

Fill in where you live now and every listing is compared with it: travel time, shops, park, noise, neighbourhood, rent and score. A postcode is enough; a house number adds the exact address (and its official energy label once you have an EP-Online key). Saved only on this computer.

Your data

Your stars, statuses, notes, score weights, travel times and current home in one file, to keep as a backup or to move to another computer. The EP-Online key isn't included.

Listings

Searches each rental site in the towns around the stations, keeps homes inside the widest areas, reads each new ad's page, merges duplicates and re-checks homes that disappeared. Takes up to an hour or two.

Energy labels

Official labels from EP-Online (RVO), looked up per address. Needs a free API key; it's saved only on this computer (ep-online-key.txt).

How to get a key
  1. Open apikey.ep-online.nl (the form is in Dutch).
  2. Naam organisatie: leave empty or fill in your name. Type organisatie: choose Overig, geen organisatie. KvK-nummer: leave empty. E-mailadres: your email.
  3. Tick Ik ga akkoord met de voorwaarden (accept the terms) and press API-key aanvragen.
  4. Within 24 hours, click the activation link in the email. The page that opens shows your key only once: copy it.
  5. Paste it below and press Save key. A new key starts working about 5 minutes after you receive it; then press Look up energy labels now.
